# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
type, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

* Being kind and respectful of other people, cultures, and viewpoints
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Taking responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
* Focusing on what is best for the community, not just for ourselves

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information (such as a photo or contact details)
  without explicit permission
* Contacting maintainers about private data leaks or security issues in a
  public thread instead of the private channel described in `SECURITY.md`

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the community team responsible for enforcement via GitHub
Discussions (private contact request) or by mentioning the repository owner
`@LuuMoss`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly, and the team will respect the privacy of the reporter.

Maintenance team guidelines in short: enforce retroactively where needed,
communicate reasons for moderation decisions when reasonable, and err on the
side of welcome for newcomers.
